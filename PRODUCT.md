# E-wise

## Status and scope

UI design prototype. Preserve the supplied mockup and wait for the user's instruction before adding pages or redesigning existing screens. Keep future edits consistent with the incumbent visual system. No backend, valuation engine, live marketplace, or transaction implementation has been requested yet.

## Confirmed product purpose

Help users answer: “What is this device still worth, and what should I do with it?” Then let them complete the suitable transaction on E-wise.

## Core workflow

Evaluate → Classify → Route → Transact.

Evaluation considers device type, brand/model, age, physical and functional condition, repairability, estimated resale and salvage values, and market demand.

| Classification | Route | On-platform action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Resellable | Resell | List usable electronics at a fixed price or receive bids and accept offers |
| Repairable / Reusable | Repair / Reuse | Book partner technicians or repair services |
| Salvageable | Salvage | Sell valuable parts or non-working devices at a fixed price or through bidding |
| Disposable | Dispose | Arrange disposal with junk shops, scrap buyers, or recycling partners |
